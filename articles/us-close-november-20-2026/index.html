Nov 21, 2026 · US Close

US Close | November 20, 2026

Chip stocks broke on Friday, November 20. The Philadelphia Semiconductor Index lost more than a tenth of its value in a single session. Treasuries rallied hard, led by short and intermediate maturities.

The S&P 500 and the Dow both closed more than 10% below their records, which puts them in correction. The Nasdaq Composite gave back all of its 2026 gain. Tech was already down more than 5% by late morning, CNBC reported. Then a Reuters report, time-stamped 2:13 p.m. ET, said the Securities and Exchange Commission is examining potential accounting issues at Nvidia, and the selling got heavier.

THE CLOSE

Rates. On the Treasury’s official par yield curve, the 2-year was 4.63%, down 13 basis points from 4.76% on November 19. The 10-year was 4.92%, down 12 basis points from 5.04%. The 3-year fell the most, down 16 basis points. The 2s10s spread widened by one basis point, to 29.

FX. The ICE dollar index was 103.860 at 4 p.m. New York time, down 0.027%, according to Yonhap Infomax. Barchart showed a 0.07% decline, so the exact move is not independently verified. The yen was the stronger haven. USD/JPY fell 0.737% to 158.494.

Commodities. January Brent settled at $92.57 a barrel, up 59 cents, or 0.64%. December WTI, which expired on the day, settled at $83.98, up 77 cents, or 0.93%. Both benchmarks still posted a third straight weekly loss.

Equities.

  • S&P 500: 6,910.39, down 265.44 points, or 3.70%
  • Nasdaq Composite: 22,617.73, down 1,261.47 points, or 5.28%
  • Dow Jones Industrial Average: 48,147.33, down 949.73 points, or 1.93%
  • PHLX Semiconductor Index (SOX): 8,408.59, down 1,011.13 points, or 10.73%
  • Cboe Volatility Index (VIX): 35.89, up 7.45 points

Index levels are official 4 p.m. ET closes. Treasury yields come from the Treasury’s end of day par curve, not intraday quotes.

WHAT CHANGED

Nvidia closed at $125.53, down 14.01%. It was already down more than 6% before the Reuters report, which cited two people briefed on the matter. Reuters said it could not determine when the inquiry began or which practices are under review. The SEC declined to comment. An investigation is not a finding of wrongdoing. The stock is also still carrying the leadership crisis that followed Jensen Huang’s exit as chief executive.

The damage did not stay with one name. Advanced Micro Devices fell 13.25% to $424.39. Micron fell 13.89% to $594.51. Broadcom fell 12.19% to $304.41. Anthropic, one session after its Nasdaq debut, fell 8.50% to $88.91, still above its $83.00 offering price. The Nasdaq-100 dropped 5.61%.

For the week ended November 20, the SOX lost 17.13%, the Nasdaq 10.02%, the S&P 500 8.61% and the Dow 8.02%.

WHERE THE SIGNAL STOPS

An official NYSE advance and decline count for the session was not available at publication, so it is left out. The split was clear anyway. Yahoo Finance counted 71 of the 101 Nasdaq-100 stocks in bear market territory, including all 20 chip and memory names. Walmart rose 1.52% to $127.95, and CNBC reported gains in Costco, Johnson & Johnson and Procter & Gamble.

The Fed path barely moved. CME FedWatch still priced a better than 90% chance of a quarter point hike in December, a little lower than on November 19, CNBC and Yonhap Infomax reported. The University of Michigan’s final November survey cut one-year inflation expectations to 3.7%, from 4.3% in the preliminary reading.

RYZM’s reading: Short-dated Treasuries rallied harder than the long end. The dollar barely moved. Oil firmed. Equities sold the AI complex and bought staples. That looks like a valuation and governance shock concentrated in one sector, more than a broad growth scare.

THE KOREA LINK

The link runs through chips and the won. KOSPI closed November 20 down 5.21%, before the SOX fell 10.73% in New York. Onshore USD/KRW traded at 1,346.00 at 4:27 a.m. KST on November 21, up 9.8 won from the 1,336.2 Seoul day close, Yonhap Infomax reported. That is an overnight quote, not an official close. In the night session, 3-year Korea Treasury Bond futures rose 20 ticks to 103.99 and 10-year futures rose 57 ticks to 110.00.

AFTER THE BELL

No major scheduled earnings or US data release after the November 20 close could be verified.

  • November 25, 8:30 a.m. ET: second estimate of third quarter GDP, plus October personal income and outlays, BEA
  • November 26: Bank of Korea rate decision. US stock markets closed for Thanksgiving
  • November 27: US stocks close early at 1 p.m. ET
