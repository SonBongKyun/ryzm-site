CrowdStrike agrees to acquire Zscaler in $43.9B all-stock deal as TSMC reaffirms intact AI demand

CrowdStrike announced an all-stock acquisition of cybersecurity peer Zscaler valued at $43.9 billion alongside its latest earnings release. Nov 23, 16:52 KST Rates CBS News

Bessent says the Fed could make a policy mistake by overtightening after a sharp tightening in financial conditions

Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent told CBS's "Face the Nation" on Nov. 22 that recent remarks from Fed officials had been very hawkish and that many people think overtightening would be a policy mistake. He said financial conditions have already tightened substantially over the past few months, and he listed that, together with political uncertainty over the AI buildout, among the causes of last week's equity selloff.

Public pressure from the Treasury on the Fed shows up first in pricing for the December meeting and at the front end of the Treasury curve. Watch fed funds futures, 2-year yields, credit spreads and other measures of financial conditions, and whether Fed speakers acknowledge the tightening that has already happened.
Nov 23, 16:38 KST Korea AP

Kospi drops 3.3% as chipmakers slide, even as U.S. stock futures bounce

Asian markets mostly fell on Monday after Friday's AI-led selloff took the S&P 500 down 3.7%. South Korea's Kospi lost 3.3% to close at 4,708.54, with Samsung Electronics down 3.1% and SK Hynix down 2.5%, even though both had recovered from steeper intraday losses. In early Monday trading, S&P 500 futures were up 2.1%.

Korea's exposure to the AI trade runs through two big memory makers and the foreign money that owns them. Watch foreign net selling of Korean equities, USD/KRW and onshore dollar funding, and whether authorities actually use the stabilization tools they discussed over the weekend.

Nov 22, 23:23 KST Korea Bloomberg via Moneycontrol

Seoul, Beijing and Taipei roll out market-calming steps after Friday's Wall Street slide

South Korea's top financial officials held an emergency meeting on Sunday and announced phased measures to steady markets, including keeping a stock stabilization fund ready to deploy if needed. China's exchanges, with regulator approval, will cut the minimum margin requirement for leveraged stock purchases to 80% from 100% on Nov. 30, a change Xinhua said could free up nearly 400 billion yuan. Taiwan had already widened curbs on short selling after its benchmark posted its worst weekly drop in 35 years.

Policy backstops in Korea work through liquidity and positioning rather than rates. Watch whether the stabilization fund is actually activated, foreign investors' net flows in Korean equities, and how USD/KRW and onshore dollar funding behave while regional authorities lean against the selloff.
Nov 22, 20:33 KST Funding Reuters

Reuters: Anthropic set tight terms for the banks on its record IPO

Reuters, citing people close to the deal, reported that Anthropic told some of its 22 underwriters to keep their deal teams to four senior bankers, largely out of concern about leaks. The sources described a compressed timeline with late changes and rushed document approvals, even as the banks earned more than half a billion dollars in fees on the offering.

Mega-cap AI listings are reshaping how equity capital gets raised. Watch the terms issuers impose on underwriters and how much new supply the primary market can absorb, since that sets the funding path for the next large AI raise.
Nov 22, 16:01 KST Credit Apollo

Apollo and Goldman price about $44 billion of notes to finance SpaceX's GPU purchases through a lease vehicle

Apollo and Goldman Sachs said on Nov. 20 they priced roughly $44 billion of senior and subordinated notes to buy GPUs and related equipment that SpaceX will lease, calling it the largest GPU financing to date. The deal was oversubscribed and upsized, with BlackRock, Goldman Sachs Asset Management and KKR-advised accounts as anchors. Lease schedules are expected to start as the equipment is delivered in the first half of 2027.

Lease-backed, off-balance-sheet structures move AI capex into the investment-grade credit market. Watch the spread these notes need over ordinary high-grade paper and how rating agencies treat collateral that ages as fast as chips do.
Nov 21, 06:23 KST Funding Oracle

Oracle says its OpenAI contract is fully funded and targets more than $30 billion of free cash flow in FY2029

In a Nov. 20 statement, Oracle said a final capital raise earlier this month completed the financing outlined at its Investor Day, including vendor financing. It said further talks with vendors are optional and are not required to deliver the OpenAI contract. The company said it continues to expect positive free cash flow in the second half of fiscal 2028 and is targeting more than $30 billion in fiscal 2029.

AI infrastructure spending is a funding question first. Watch how hyperscalers close the gap between capital outlays and free cash flow, not the headline size of the contract.
Nov 21, 06:07 KST Credit Bloomberg

CrowdStrike is in talks to acquire Zscaler, people familiar with the matter tell Bloomberg

Bloomberg, citing people familiar with the matter, said the companies are discussing a combination and that a deal is not certain. Zscaler’s market value was $32 billion at Friday’s regular close, Bloomberg reported.

A large software combination would be a financing event in equity and credit markets. Watch whether any agreed deal, if one is reached, is funded with cash, stock, or new issuance.
Nov 21, 04:49 KST Korea Barron's

Barron's: Google cuts planned 2027 shipments of Broadcom-designed AI chips

Barron's reported that Google has cut planned 2027 shipments of custom chips developed with Broadcom. The paper said Broadcom shares fell more than 10% on Friday.

Revisions to custom-accelerator build plans move through the memory and packaging chain. Watch Korea’s export data and HBM order books as those plans are updated.
Nov 21, 02:18 KST Rates Federal Reserve

Fed's Cook says inflation is well above target and dissented in favor of another hike

In a Nov. 20 speech, Governor Lisa Cook said inflation has been above the Fed’s 2% target for 67 months, with October PCE estimated at 3.8% and core PCE at 3.2%. She dissented from the October decision to hold the federal funds rate at 3.75% to 4%, preferring another quarter-point increase.

Watch the policy-rate path and real yields as officials weigh whether current settings are tight enough while inflation remains above target.
Nov 21, 00:03 KST Rates University of Michigan

Michigan final sentiment rises to 51.5 as year-ahead inflation expectations fall to 3.7%

The University of Michigan’s final November Consumer Sentiment Index was 51.5, up 7.3% from 48.0 in October. Median year-ahead inflation expectations fell to 3.7% from 4.7%, and longer-run expectations moved down to 3.3% from 3.5%.

Inflation expectations are an input into the real-rate path. Watch the 3.7% year-ahead reading alongside official PCE as the Fed debates the policy setting.
