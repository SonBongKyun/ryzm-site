RYZM Daily Brief | US CPI, TSMC revenue and the Korea close | November 11, 2026

Sources checked 11 November 2026 at 09:16 KST. Daily checks are scheduled for 09:00 KST. Market figures refer to the 10 November regular sessions.

US inflation | October CPI rose 0.4%

Headline CPI rose 0.4% and core CPI 0.3% month on month, seasonally adjusted. Headline CPI rose 0.6% in September. BLS published the report on 10 November at 22:30 KST. Headline and core year-on-year rates were unavailable because the October 2025 comparison data were missing. October 2026 US Consumer Price Index.

Semiconductors | TSMC October sales

TSMC reported consolidated revenue of NT$586.89 billion, up 14.7% from September and 59.7% from a year earlier. The company release is dated 10 November in Taiwan and gives no publication time. TSMC October 2026 consolidated revenue.

Market closes | 10 November

At the 10 November regular-session closes, the KOSPI rose 4.39% to 5,239.13. In the US session, the S&P 500 fell 0.4%, the Dow fell 0.3% and the Nasdaq Composite fell 0.7%. KOSPI regular-session close for 10 November 2026 was published on 10 November at 17:49 KST. US equity index closes for 10 November 2026 covers the US trading date. Its exact publication time was unavailable.

What to watch

The US October PPI is scheduled for 13 November at 22:30 KST. Compare producer-price pressures with the consumer-price report. For TSMC, look for margin and revenue-mix detail in the next earnings release. BLS November 2026 release calendar.
