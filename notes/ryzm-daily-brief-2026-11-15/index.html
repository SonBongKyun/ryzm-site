RYZM Weekend Brief | Samara oil hub and the week ahead | November 15, 2026

Sources checked 15 November 2026 at 09:10 KST. Daily checks are scheduled for 09:00 KST. Korean market figures are from 13 November.

Ukraine’s security service reports strike on Russian oil pipeline hub

Ukraine’s security service said on 14 November that its drones struck an oil dispatch station near Prosvet, in Russia’s Samara region, on 13 November. It reported preliminary damage to storage tanks. The statement identifies the Samara station as a site where crude is blended for export as Urals. The extent of damage and any change in export flows were unavailable at the review. Security Service of Ukraine statement, posted 14 November at 12:05 as displayed (time zone unspecified).

Watch for a statement from the pipeline operator or verified changes in shipment volumes.
